What happens when you use the form?

The form runs locally in your browser. Pressing “Prepare my request” opens your device's email application with a prefilled message. The website does not transmit your form data to ProspectPilot automatically, and it does not store it in a website database. You decide whether to send the email.

Information you choose to send

If you send the prepared email, it may include your name, work email, agency name, target industry and location, and any notes you provide. ProspectPilot uses that information to evaluate and respond to your enquiry, and to communicate about requested services.

Email provider and retention

Messages are processed by the email services used by the sender and ProspectPilot. Information may be retained for correspondence and business record purposes. Specific retention periods and legal obligations will be established before full commercial launch.

Third-party services

This website loads fonts from Google Fonts. Your browser may contact Google's servers to retrieve them. The website does not intentionally deploy analytics, advertising pixels, or tracking cookies in this version.

Your questions

To request access, correction, or deletion of information you emailed, contact prospectpilot.contact@gmail.com. Requests are subject to applicable legal requirements.
